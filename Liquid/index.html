Story

Behind Liquid Theory

Liquid Theory started as a simple question: what if drink menus were designed the way good products are designed — clear, repeatable and easy to run on any “hardware” (your kitchen, your bar, your venue)?

Each recipe lives as a spec: glassware, ratios, garnish, prep steps and even backup ingredients if something’s missing. That’s how we keep your favourite drink consistent from night to night.

Whether it’s a dry event, a launch party or a small tasting at home, we treat your menu like a mini-product: tested, iterated and shipped with documentation.

Modular menus Mix 0% ABV, low-ABV and classic cocktails on one board with clear labelling for guests.
Compact mise-en-place Ingredients are shared across multiple drinks, so you carry fewer bottles and waste less.
Event playbooks Shopping lists, prep timelines and on-the-night checklists so you or your bartender stay relaxed.
